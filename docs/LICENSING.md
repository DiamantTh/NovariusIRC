# Licensing history

## Current license

NovariusIRC is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3
or any later version (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). The complete license text is in the
repository root at `LICENSE`.

## Why the project changed licenses

The earlier Novara Software Freedom License (NSFL) grew out of Thomas
(DiamantTh)'s own long-running thoughts and frustrations about proprietary
control, platform power, artificial lock-in, opaque services, and the
commercial exploitation of free software. It attempted to combine technical
software freedom with fair economic use and broader social responsibility.
Those ideas were also closely connected to the wider Novara/SVZ concept of a
transparent and values-based political and institutional order.

In retrospect, that approach asked a software license to perform tasks that go
beyond licensing source code and would require a broader social, political,
and institutional framework. No state or public institution stands behind the
bespoke NSFL to safeguard those wider PMPC-oriented and values-based aims as a
coherent legal framework. It also made the legal terms harder for users,
packagers, and contributors to recognize and evaluate.

The project therefore moved to `AGPL-3.0-or-later`. The AGPL addresses the
central requirement for this network-facing project with an established free
software license: modified versions used to provide a network service must
offer their corresponding source code to the users of that service.

The wider ethical and political ideas behind the NSFL remain documented as
project history and in `VALUES.md`, but they do not impose additional
restrictions on the rights granted by the AGPL.

## Historical license text

The former NSFL text is retained unchanged under
`licenses/legacy/Novara-Software-Freedom-License-EN.md` for historical and
documentary purposes. It is not the current license of NovariusIRC.

Copies of older releases remain subject to the license terms under which those
copies were originally received. The current repository and releases made
after the change use `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.
