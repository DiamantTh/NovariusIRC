# Project values

NovariusIRC is free software licensed under the GNU Affero General Public
License, version 3 or any later version. The rights to use, study, modify, and
share the software come exclusively from that license.

The project additionally values:

- transparent and auditable software;
- open standards and practical interoperability;
- self-hosting, portability, and meaningful exit options;
- privacy, accessibility, and respectful community participation;
- public software that remains available to the public;
- resistance to artificial lock-in and opaque platform control.

We ask users and operators to apply NovariusIRC in ways consistent with human
rights, public accountability, and the principles expressed by the
"Public Money? Public Code!" initiative.

This document is a non-binding statement of project values. It does not add
conditions to or restrict the rights granted by the AGPL.
